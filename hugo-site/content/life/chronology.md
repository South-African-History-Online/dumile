Zwelidumile Jeremiah Mxqasi – (Dumile Feni)

Osman Utletaan Feni

B. 21 / 5/ 1942. D. 16/10/1991 New York

## 1960

Exhibits work at the Open Air Fair in Joubert Park. Johannesburg.

## 1963

Dumile admitted to Charles Hurwitz (Santa ) South African National Tuberculosis Association hospital in Johannesburg.

Meet the artists Ngatane at the hospital encouraged by Mrs. Forster the wife of the hospital superintendent they painted a number of murals in the hospital.

## 1964

Dumile joins classes run by Ngatane at the Chiawelo Centre in Soweto. Has his portrait painted by Ngatane. Portrait in the MTN collection.

## 1965

Exhibits work at the Transvaal Academy annual show, held at the Johannesburg Art Gallery.

## 1966

Dumile’s first solo exhibition opens at Gallery 101 – Johannesburg.

January.

Exhibition opens at the Durban Municipal Art Gallery on the 18 August and ran until the 5<sup>th</sup> September.

0n 25<sup>th</sup> October, Trans- Natal group show initiated by Omar Badsha and Dumile opens at the newly opened Natal Society Of Arts Gallery. The exhibition closed on the 5<sup>th</sup> November.

Republic Festival Exhibition held in Pretoria, to celebrate the fifth anniversary of the founding of the white republic, organised by the white only Northern Transvaal region of the South African Association of Arts.

The annual SA Breweries Art Prize. Wins merit award for mother and child.

Pretoria Art Museum

Johannesburg Civic Theatre

Group show at the South African pavilion at expo 67 in Montreal.

Dumile work showed at the 13 Anniversary of the Transvaal Academy annual exhibition held at the Adler Fielding Gallery. November 1966 (Notice in RDM 10-11-1966)

## 1967

Solo exhibition, opened by Mary Harari. Gallery 101.

9 June - Gallery 101 Johannesburg.

## 1968

Participate in the annual Transvaal Academy exhibition.

One of a number of artists representing South Africa at the Sao Paulo Biennale, Brazil.

Work exhibited at the Adler Fielding Galleries in Exhibition titled “

” Sculpture South Africa 1900 – 1967.

Solo exhibition Titled “Sketches from a private collection” Goodman Gallery, Johannesburg.

July, Dumile leaves South Africa to attend exhibition in London.

Group Show held at the South African Association of Arts Gallery. Cape Town exhibition of the work of Dumile, Badsha, Ben Arnold Durant Sihlali, G Sekoto, Erick Mbata, Ben Macala … 27 August.

1969 Group show titled Contemporary African Art, Camden Arts Centre, London.

Grosvenor gallery, London. August – Sept 8<sup>th</sup>.

1970 Represented in group show Titled “The 51 Club Winter Art Exhibition, Goodman gallery. Johannesburg.

Solo exhibition. Work from the collection of Desmond Fisher, Goodman Gallery. Johannesburg.

## 1971

Represented in group show, Gallery 101. Johannesburg

Wins first price for sculpture African Studies centre, University of California, Los Angeles.

## 1972

Wins the UTA Pavement Competition. USA

1974 Goodman Galleries – group show 24<sup>th</sup> January 1974.

1975 Goodman Gallery (SA Sculpture)

Group show “African Art from SA” Gallery 21, London

1977 Gallery 21,

Group show held at the South African National Gallery, as part of the annual Cape Town Festival.

Group show, Contemporary African Art in SA. Organised by the University of Fort Hare, which travels to Rand Afrikaans University, Pretoria Museum, University of Orange Free State, William Hamphrey Art Gallery.

1981 Group show Black Art Today, Jabulani Standard Bank, Soweto

1982 Group show, Exhibition of SA Art, National Museum and Art Gallery, as part of the Art Towards Social Development festival, Gaborone, Botswana

1980- 83

Attended New York University, Graduate programme in film and television.

## 1980

Exhibition at Los Angeles African-American Museum.

Participates in exhibition marking the 25<sup>th</sup> anniversary celebrations of the Freedom Charter at the United Nations Plaza.

## 1983

U.N. Exhibition commemoration of Namibia Freedom Day, New York City.

## 1986

Group show, City Without walls Gallery, Newark, New Jersey

Visiting lecturer in the department of Fine Art at UCLA and the Massachusetts College of Art.

1987 Unlock Apartheid's Jails, conference on children under apartheid,

with Bill Cosby and The American Committee of Africa, Hyatt Hotel, New York City

Mural for the University of California, Los Angeles

## 1988

Group show “voices from exile” exhibition. U.S. tour including Washington DC, Los Angeles, Houston, Philadelphia and other cities. For the American Committee on Africa's Unlock Apartheid Jails' campaign

Work exhibited, Triptych.

Solo Exhibition La galleria, New York, USA

Solo exhibition “Statements Dumile” (date venue unknown)

Uhuru: an Exhibition of African American Art Against Apartheid. City Without Wall Gallery, Newark, New Jersey, USA.

Voices From Exile, USA, (Seven SA Artists), Washington DC, Los

Angeles, Houston, Philadelphia.

Completes portrait of Nelson Mandela for the Pathfinder Mural, N.Y.C.

1990:

Township Art from SA, Applecrest, New York, USA

1991 Dies in New York 16/10/1991

Buried in Muslim cemetery in Lenasia, Johannesburg South Africa.

Memorial service held at the premises of the Johannesburg Art Foundation speakers Wally Serote, Nadine Gordimer and Ezrom Legae speakers. (star 1 Nov).

1991 Solo exhibition at the Grahamestown Festival. Grahamestown. July

1992 Grahamestown Festival exhibition opens at Technikon Natal Gallery. Durban. March

2003 The South African Government awards posthumous award the Order of Ikhamanga in Gold.

## 2004

Formation of Dumile Feni Foundation. Johannesburg

Formation of Dumile Family Trust. Johannesburg.

Solo exhibition Artists in Exile an exhibition and catalogue by Bruce Smith. Art on Paper Gallery. Melville.

## 2005

Dumile retrospective exhibition Johannesburg Art Gallery. 30 January

Awards
